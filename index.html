Who we are

Empowering Filipino entrepreneurs. Creating lasting impact.

Markadong Pinoy is a full-service franchise consulting and business solutions group built for modern Filipino entrepreneurs — especially OFWs — who want to build, grow, and scale businesses without the burden of daily operations. Backed by seasoned professionals, mentors, and industry stakeholders, we help build businesses that are structured, scalable, compliant, and sustainable.

Our mark is a tribute to that mission: the Philippine sun for a sunrise of new beginnings, a central circle for unity and community, and an “M” shaped like an open book for transparency and shared knowledge. Together, the sun and the “P” trace an infinity symbol — boundless possibilities and lasting growth.
